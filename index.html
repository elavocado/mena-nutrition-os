YOUR SIMPLE SYSTEM

How to make this work for you

A small set of practical rules. Nothing complicated.

🧩

Pick by situation

No Monday-to-Sunday meal assignments. Open Breakfast, Snacks, Lunch, Dinner or In a rush and choose what suits your time and appetite.

The + button adds that recipe's ingredients to your one weekly Lidl list. The − button removes a selection.

🍝

Rice, pasta or wedges. Cook once, eat twice.

Pick one easy carb: 2 rice scoops, 2 fifths of a 500 g Combino pasta bag (about 200 g dry), or ready-cut wedges. Add 2 fish/chicken portions or 400 g lean mince plus one 300 g vegetable carton. Split into two main meals.

Only boil pasta in your rice cooker if its manual supports it. Air-fry raw meat or fish separately unless your cooker is explicitly designed to cook it safely. Chill the second meal promptly.

🥬

Lactose and legumes

Ordinary fresh dairy is not part of this menu. Low-fat cheese slices are optional and should be lactose-free or known to suit you. Aged Gouda often contains very little lactose, but tolerance varies.

Freshona Buttergemüse has butter and peas. Butter is usually low in lactose; peas may cause digestive symptoms. Read the carton and choose plain vegetables if necessary.

🌱

Regular eating, gentle approach

Keep breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks available without prescribing the same food every day. Long gaps and rigid diet rules can worsen loss-of-control eating for some people. This isn't a calorie target or weight-loss prescription.

If binge eating is recurring, guided self-help or eating-disorder-focused CBT with a qualified professional can help.

⚠️ Food safety: Follow package instructions, check meat and fish are safely cooked, and chill cooked rice promptly. Reheat leftovers thoroughly only once. If an air-fryer basket is crowded, cook in batches.

A personal meal-organizing tool, not a medical diagnosis or a clinician-approved diet. Background: NHS eating support, IQWiG lactose guide, Lidl Buttergemüse.
