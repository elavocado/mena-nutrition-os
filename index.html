YOUR QUICK-START GUIDE

Build it from ready-made units

Very little cutting, no daily weighing and no pressure to finish a plate.

🧩

One batch = two meals

For a rice day: 2 individually portioned salmon fillets + your 100 g dry-rice scoop + one 300 g Buttergemüse carton. Split in half. You can eat both portions on the same day or the next day if stored safely.

  • Check your scoop once: volume markings aren't guaranteed to equal dry-rice weight.
  • Remove every plastic wrapper before cooking.
  • Buttergemüse has peas. Choose a pea-free mix if legumes cause symptoms.
🍚

Rice cooker safety

Use rice and vegetables in the pot. Raw fish or chicken belongs in a suitable steamer basket only when your cooker is designed for it. Otherwise cook the protein in the air fryer.

  • Never assume the rice cycle cooks raw meat sufficiently.
  • Check core temperature and follow package directions.
  • Chill the second portion promptly; rice leftovers are especially time-sensitive.
♨️

Air fryer, no knife

Use ready-cut frozen potato wedges. Add fish, chicken, minced beef or an egg dish at the right time. A crowded basket needs two rounds.

  • Chicken about 74°C, ground beef about 71°C, fish about 63°C as practical safety checks.
  • Eggs fully set; follow appliance guidance.
  • For ready-seasoned wedges, extra oil may be unnecessary.
🌱

Regular eating first

Example rhythm: 09:00 breakfast, 11:30 snack, 14:00 lunch, 17:00 snack, 20:00 dinner. Adjust to your day and hunger. This replaces the old two-meal fasting structure.

Regular meals and snacks can help reduce over-hunger and loss-of-control eating. Rigid dieting can make binge eating harder to treat. A therapist can help with guided self-help or CBT for eating difficulties.

💚 Reset rule: If one eating moment goes differently, keep the next one regular. No fasting to make up for it. This is a food organization tool, not a calorie deficit prescription. Always cool cooked rice quickly, store cold, and reheat thoroughly only once.
