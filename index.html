YOUR QUICK-START GUIDE

Make the machines do the work

Actual cooking times depend on your appliance and portion size.

🍚

Rice cooker

Rice and vegetables go in the pot. Salmon or chicken can go in a steaming tray only if your model supports cooking them safely. Follow its water markings.

  • Add canned tuna after the rice finishes.
  • Chicken must reach 74°C inside; salmon 63°C.
  • Never leave cooked rice cooling in the cooker.
♨️

Air fryer

Start small potato cubes at about 190°C for 12–15 minutes, then add the protein and vegetables at the right time for your basket and cut sizes.

  • Ground beef must reach 71°C internally.
  • Cook eggs and egg dishes until fully set.
  • Times are guides; a food thermometer matters.
🥣

Your eating rhythm

12:00 first meal, 15:30 Skyr-and-banana snack, 19:00 second meal.

Times are flexible. Eat earlier or add enough food when you're genuinely hungry. The plan shouldn't force excessive restriction or fasting.

🌱

Numbers are guides

Roughly 2,200–2,400 kcal and around 120–160 g protein per day, depending on products and preparation. These are estimates, not a strict calorie prescription.

Regular eating and comfortable fullness matter more than hitting exact numbers.

⚠️ Rice safety: Cool cooked rice promptly, refrigerate and use within 24 hours, or freeze it. Reheat until steaming hot, only once. Use a thermometer for meat and fish, especially when steaming raw proteins.
